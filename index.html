Active / Stage 1 & 2 Complete • B.Tech Final Year Major Project

G+2 RESIDENTIAL RCC BUILDING: 3D STAAD.PRO ANALYSIS & BENTLEY RCDC DESIGN

Plot Footprint 33' × 65' (2,145 sq. ft.)
Configuration G+2 (Height < 10m)
3D FEA Engine Bentley STAAD.Pro 2025
Design Standard IS 456:2000 • SP 34 (Zone II)

End-to-end computational engineering execution of an East-facing multi-storey residential space frame in Mahabubnagar, Telangana (Seismic Zone II, Z = 0.10). The project delivers a rigorous, site-constructable pipeline from statutory setback compliance (Telangana G.O. Ms. No. 168) and empirical geotechnical soil testing, to 3D space frame finite element analysis and automated limit state concrete detailing without rebar congestion.

  • Statutory Planning (G.O. Ms. No. 168) — 33' × 65' East-facing plot engineered strictly within the 10.0m height ceiling (G+2) with mandatory 1.5m front and 1.0m rear/side ventilation setbacks.
  • Empirical Geotechnical SBC — Physical laboratory soil testing establishing site-specific Safe Bearing Capacity for isolated reinforced concrete pad footings.
  • 3D FEA Analysis (STAAD.Pro 2025) — Complete 3D skeletal space frame modeled and analyzed under Dead Loads (IS 875 Part 1), Live Loads (IS 875 Part 2), and Lateral Seismic Forces for Zone II (IS 1893:2016, Z = 0.10).
  • Automated Limit State Detailing (Bentley RCDC 2021) — Full concrete design and constructable detailing per IS 456:2000 & SP 34 across columns, beams, and slabs with automated BBS and BOQ extraction.
